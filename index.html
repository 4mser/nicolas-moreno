// 02 — Field notes

How I ended up here.

I grew up in Santiago. In 2019 I started Physics Engineering at Universidad Andrés Bello because I wanted to understand everything: what we are, where we're headed, and why things are the way they are. Physics was the only thing I felt could give some shape to all those questions spinning around in my head.

When the pandemic hit, I moved to Valdivia. I spent six years there doing software and design, learning by building things, breaking them, and trying again. Since 2022 I've worked on this full-time: systems, data analysis, artificial intelligence, and automation.

What I still love most is that moment when an idea that existed only in my head starts running on its own.

Outside of work, I watch a lot of films and series, listen to music, and read a lot. I also try to spend as much time as I can in nature and with the people I love. I'm still amazed by how strange, how chaotic, and above all how beautiful and full of life this incredible rock floating in space is — and by the luck of being here, conscious, to see it.

In 2026 I came back to Santiago. I came back with more experience, more ideas, and more hunger than ever… in every sense.
